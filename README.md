"ONE DAY · ONE ORBIT" — one poster that sums up the world's news of **Tuesday 17 November 2026**. A hero panel carries the day's biggest story, Ukraine's missile-and-drone strike that partly collapsed the Kerch (Crimea) Bridge, with Russia striking Kyiv's bridges in reply. Six tiles orbit it: Trump in Manila for the Quad summit ahead of the first US–China–Russia leaders' summit in Shenzhen; Iranian drones targeting the US base in Bahrain and a tanker hitting a mine in the Strait of Hormuz; the UN's Gaza winter warning (1.7M displaced) as the UK and allies demanded aid crossings open; California suing OpenAI over AI safety; the US losing its measles-elimination status after 26 years; and the COP31 draft deal in Antalya calling for a faster fossil-fuel phase-out and $120bn in climate aid. The program at `main.py` copies the shipped `entry.png` to the output directory byte for byte — no network, fully deterministic.
